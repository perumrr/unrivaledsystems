See what we do

Good hardware doesn't stop working
just because a new model comes out

Every day, millions of computer parts get thrown out: upgraded, cleared from an office, or sold because someone doesn't know what they have. Most of it still runs fine and can be reused.

We track those listings across Michigan, buy the parts worth saving, and put them back to work instead of letting them sit in a drawer or landfill.

Unrivaled Systems exists so people can get a genuinely good computer without paying the exorbitant new hardware markup, and without adding to the pile of electronics that is destined to break down in 10 million years.

Every machine we sell is tested and stands behind itself. Not a mystery box of parts, a rebuilt system we'd use ourselves (and we do!).

Build Process

Find the parts

We watch listings for all parts and cases that are priced fair and worth a second life, with the part's previous history in mind.

Match and assemble

Parts are matched for compatibility and balanced performance, then assembled into a clean, cable managed system.

Pair and build

Every part is checked on its own. Power, temps, stability before it's even listed.

Hand off

Finished builds run a stress test before they ever reach a buyer, so what you get is a finished, reliable build.
