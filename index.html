Good hardware doesn't stop working
just because someone lists it for parts.

Every day, working CPUs, GPUs, and motherboards get listed for cheap — upgraded out, cleared from an office, or sold because someone doesn't know what they have. Most of it still runs fine.

We track those listings across Michigan and beyond, buy the parts worth saving, and put them back to work instead of letting them sit in a drawer or a landfill.

Unrivaled Systems exists so people can get a genuinely good computer without paying new-hardware markup, and without adding to the pile of electronics nobody's using.

Every machine we sell is tested and stands behind itself — not a mystery box of parts, a rebuilt system we'd use ourselves.

What we do

Three things, done in this order, every time.

Source

We buy individual parts — not pre-built systems — from Facebook Marketplace, eBay, and local sellers around Michigan.

Rebuild

Parts are cleaned, matched for compatibility, and assembled into complete desktops built around what each buyer actually needs.

Sell direct

No storefront markup. Builds are priced around what's in them, sold straight to the person who's going to use them.

How a build comes together

Every system we sell goes through the same four steps.
01

Find the parts

We watch listings for CPUs, GPUs, motherboards, RAM, storage, and cases that are priced fair and worth a second life.

02

Test before it goes in a build

Every part is checked on its own — power, temps, stability — before it's allowed anywhere near a customer's system.

03

Match and assemble

Parts get paired for real compatibility and balanced performance, then built into a clean, cable-managed system.

04

Burn-in and hand off

Finished builds run a stress test before they ever reach a buyer, so what you get is proven, not just assembled.
